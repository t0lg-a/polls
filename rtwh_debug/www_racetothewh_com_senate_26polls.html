2026 Senate Polling

Looking for the Senate Forecast? Click Here

Track the latest polls for the 2026 Senate Elections. This page is split into three sections. Start with the general election: choose a Senate race, pick the candidates representing each party, and see who leads in the polling. Second, scroll to the primaries section to see which candidates are leading in the Democratic and Republican contests that will decide each party’s nominees. Finally, the third section tracks how popular all the potential candidates are in each state using favorability/unfavourability polling. To provide a more accurate picture of the election, our polling average gives more weight to polls from pollsters with an excellent track record, polls conducted recently, and polls with large sample sizes.

The Generic Ballot used to be tracked in this average, but now it has its own page here. It shows whether voters nationwide prefer Democrats or Republicans in the next election. Have any questions? Send an email to the RacetotheWH founder at racetothewh@gmail.com.
